## Summary & Context

In [PR #880](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/lws/pull/880) (shipped in `v0.8.0`), the `LeaderWorkerSet` CRD was moved out of `charts/lws/templates/crds/` into the top-level `charts/lws/crds/` directory so that `helm uninstall` would not delete the CRD and all `LeaderWorkerSet` custom resources in the cluster. However, when upgrading an existing Helm release from `<= v0.7.0` to `>= v0.8.0`, Helm sees the CRD disappear from the rendered `templates/` manifest and **deletes the CRD (and all existing LWS workloads)** unless the user first annotates the CRD with `helm.sh/resource-policy=keep` and reconciles `charts/lws/crds` via `kubectl apply --server-side --force-conflicts`.

This PR implements the e2e test requested in [#1049](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/lws/issues/1049) to continuously verify the documented `v0.7.0 -> current` Helm upgrade and CRD migration procedure ([`charts/lws/README.md`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/charts/lws/README.md#L72-L120)):

1. **[`Makefile`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/Makefile#L208-L222)**: Adds the `test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy` target (`LWS_UPGRADE_METHOD=helm LWS_UPGRADE_LEGACY_CRD=true LWS_UPGRADE_FROM_VERSION=v0.7.0`) and appends it to `test-e2e-upgrade`.
2. **[`hack/e2e-test.sh`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L26-L31)**: Adds `LWS_UPGRADE_LEGACY_CRD` handling to annotate `leaderworkersets.leaderworkerset.x-k8s.io` with `helm.sh/resource-policy=keep` prior to `helm upgrade` in [`upgrade_to_current_helm`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L200-L224), forwards `LWS_UPGRADE_LEGACY_CRD` to Ginkgo in [`run_upgrade_phase`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L226-L237), and excludes `disaggregatedsets` from artifact collection in [`cleanup`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L74-L82).
3. **[`test/e2e/upgrade/suite_test.go`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/suite_test.go#L46-L66) & [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L83-L325)**: Reads `LWS_UPGRADE_LEGACY_CRD`, adapts pre-upgrade assertions for `v0.7.0` (`ObservedGeneration == 0` via [`expectLegacyLeaderWorkerSetAvailable`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L179-L197) and skipping `ControllerRevision` hash comparison), skips `DisaggregatedSet` checks, and verifies post-upgrade that the `LeaderWorkerSet` CRD survived with `helm.sh/resource-policy: keep` ([`expectLeaderWorkerSetCRDKept`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L120-L129)).

## Overall Assessment

The PR cleanly reproduces and guards against the `v0.7.0 -> current` CRD deletion regression, and the workarounds for `v0.7.0` vs. Kubernetes 1.34+ `ControllerRevision` hashing (`creationTimestamp: null` removal) and unset `condition.ObservedGeneration` are well-reasoned and appropriately scoped to the upgrade test suite.

However, there are **two main issues** worth addressing before merging (or in a quick follow-up), plus a couple of minor test-coverage and documentation nits:

1. **Medium (CI Architecture / Artifact Clobbering)**: Adding `test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy` to the aggregate `test-e2e-upgrade` target causes Prow's `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-main` job to sequentially spin up and tear down **3 separate Kind clusters**, rebuild the controller image 3 times, duplicate the entire `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-helm-main` Prow job, and **overwrite all log/JUnit/snapshot artifacts in `$ARTIFACTS`** on each run.
2. **Medium (Post-Upgrade Coverage)**: [`waitForWebhooks`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L138-L140), [`createNewWorkloads`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L206-L208), and [`cleanup`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L76-L81) skip `DisaggregatedSet` unconditionally whenever `legacyCRD` is true—even in `upgradePhase == "after"`. Because [`upgrade_to_current_helm`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L210-L220) applies `charts/lws/crds` (which installs the `DisaggregatedSet` CRD) and upgrades with `--set enableDisaggregatedSet=true`, the `"after"` phase *can and should* verify that `DisaggregatedSet` webhooks and workloads work after upgrading from `v0.7.0`.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. [Medium] `test-e2e-upgrade` runs 3 sequential Kind cluster cycles in one Prow job and overwrites `$ARTIFACTS`

- **Location**: [`Makefile:208-222`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/Makefile#L208-L222), [`hack/e2e-test.sh:46`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L46), [`hack/e2e-test.sh:55-85`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L55-L85), [`hack/e2e-test.sh:234-235`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L234-L235)

In [`Makefile`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/Makefile#L208-L210):
```makefile
.PHONY: test-e2e-upgrade
test-e2e-upgrade: test-e2e-upgrade-manifests test-e2e-upgrade-helm test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy
```

In Prow (`test-infra`), there are currently two upgrade presubmit jobs:
- `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-main` -> runs `make test-e2e-upgrade`
- `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-helm-main` -> runs `make test-e2e-upgrade-helm`

With this change, `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-main` executes all three targets (`test-e2e-upgrade-manifests`, `test-e2e-upgrade-helm`, and `test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy`) back-to-back in the same job, all sharing the same `ARTIFACTS` directory:
1. **Artifact Clobbering**: Every invocation of `./hack/e2e-test.sh` writes to identical filenames inside `$ARTIFACTS`:
   - `junit-upgrade-before.xml` and `junit-upgrade-after.xml` ([`hack/e2e-test.sh:234`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L234))
   - `old-controller.log` and `lws-controller-manager.log` ([`hack/e2e-test.sh:61`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L61), [`hack/e2e-test.sh:349`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L349))
   - `upgrade-snapshot.json` ([`hack/e2e-test.sh:46`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L46))
   - `upgrade-workloads.yaml` and `events.log` ([`hack/e2e-test.sh:75-81`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L75-L81))
   - `kind export logs "$ARTIFACTS"` ([`hack/e2e-test.sh:84`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L84))
   Because `test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy` runs last, it overwrites the JUnit XML reports and logs from `test-e2e-upgrade-manifests` and `test-e2e-upgrade-helm` when all succeed.
2. **Redundant CI Work & Runtime**: `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-main` now takes ~12 minutes (vs. ~4.5 minutes for `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-helm-main`), rebuilds the controller Docker image 3 times via `kind-image-build`, and runs `test-e2e-upgrade-helm` even though `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-helm-main` already runs it in parallel.

**Suggestion**:
- At minimum, write artifacts to a run-specific subdirectory (e.g., `ARTIFACTS=$(ARTIFACTS)/upgrade-manifests`, `ARTIFACTS=$(ARTIFACTS)/upgrade-helm`, `ARTIFACTS=$(ARTIFACTS)/upgrade-helm-legacy`, or suffix `junit-upgrade-*.xml` with the upgrade method/from-version) so JUnit reports and controller logs are not overwritten when `make test-e2e-upgrade` runs multiple sub-targets.
- Additionally, consider running `test-e2e-upgrade-helm-legacy` as part of `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-helm-main` (or its own Prow job) while keeping `test-e2e-upgrade-manifests` in `pull-lws-test-e2e-upgrade-main` so the two Prow jobs run balanced workloads in parallel instead of duplicating `test-e2e-upgrade-helm`.

### 2. [Medium] Post-upgrade `DisaggregatedSet` creation is skipped when `legacyCRD` is true

- **Location**: [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go:138-146`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L138-L146), [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go:206-213`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L206-L213), [`hack/e2e-test.sh:76-81`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L76-L81)

In [`upgrade_to_current_helm`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L210-L220):
```bash
    $KUBECTL apply --server-side --force-conflicts -f "$CWD/charts/lws/crds"
    ...
    $HELM upgrade "$HELM_RELEASE_NAME" "$CWD/charts/lws" \
        --namespace "$LWS_NAMESPACE" \
        --set image.manager.repository="$image_repo" \
        --set image.manager.tag="$image_tag" \
        --set image.manager.pullPolicy=IfNotPresent \
        --set enableDisaggregatedSet=true \
        --wait --timeout 5m
```
During the upgrade from `v0.7.0` to `current`, `kubectl apply --server-side --force-conflicts -f "$CWD/charts/lws/crds"` installs **both** CRDs in `charts/lws/crds/`—including `disaggregatedsets.disaggregatedset.x-k8s.io`—and `helm upgrade` enables the `DisaggregatedSet` controller and webhook via `--set enableDisaggregatedSet=true`. In fact, installing newly introduced CRDs (`DisaggregatedSet` in `v0.9.0+`) is one of the primary reasons [`charts/lws/README.md` ("Reconciling CRDs on upgrade")](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/charts/lws/README.md#L83-L96) instructs users to apply `charts/lws/crds` before `helm upgrade`.

However, because [`waitForWebhooks()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L138-L140) and [`createNewWorkloads()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L206-L208) check `if legacyCRD { return }`:
- In `upgradePhase == "before"`, skipping `DisaggregatedSet` in `waitForWebhooks()`, `createExistingWorkloads()`, and `captureSnapshot()` is necessary because `v0.7.0` predates `DisaggregatedSet`.
- In `upgradePhase == "after"`, skipping `DisaggregatedSet` in `waitForWebhooks()` and `createNewWorkloads()` misses testing that a cluster upgraded from `v0.7.0` to `current` can now reconcile a newly created `DisaggregatedSet` (`new-ds`)!

**Suggestion**:
1. In [`waitForWebhooks()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L138-L140), only skip waiting for the `DisaggregatedSet` webhook during the `"before"` phase:
   ```go
   if legacyCRD && upgradePhase == "before" {
       return
   }
   ```
2. In [`createNewWorkloads()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L206-L213) (which only runs in `upgradePhase == "after"`), remove the `if legacyCRD { return }` early return so `new-ds` is created and validated after upgrading from `v0.7.0`.
3. In [`cleanup`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/hack/e2e-test.sh#L76-L81), `disaggregatedsets` will exist at cleanup time once `upgrade_to_current_helm` has applied `charts/lws/crds` (or you can check `$KUBECTL get crd disaggregatedsets.disaggregatedset.x-k8s.io >/dev/null 2>&1`).

### 3. [Low] Pre-upgrade pod readiness and standalone Service UID are not verified/snapshotted when `legacyCRD` is true

- **Location**: [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go:156-168`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L156-L168), [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go:317-324`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L317-L324), [`test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go:395-406`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L395-L406)

When `!legacyCRD`, [`createExistingWorkloads()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L165-L167) calls `testutils.ExpectValidPods(ctx, k8sClient, lws, &corev1.PodList{})`. When `legacyCRD` is true, `ExpectValidPods` is skipped because it validates the `leaderworkerset.x-k8s.io/template-revision-hash` label against `revisionutils.NewRevision(...)` (which differs between `v0.7.0` and `main` on Kubernetes 1.34+).

While [`expectLegacyLeaderWorkerSetAvailable(lws)`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L181-L197) waits for `LeaderWorkerSetAvailable=True` and [`workloadPodSnapshots()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L395-L405) checks `len(standalonePods.Items) == 4`, `workloadPodSnapshots()` does not check `isPodReady(&pod)` on the standalone LWS pods (unlike [`waitForDisaggregatedSet`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L297-L301)), and [`captureSnapshot()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L317-L324) does not snapshot the standalone LWS headless Service UID (`existing-lws`).

**Suggestion**:
- In [`workloadPodSnapshots()`](file:///usr/local/google/home/ahg/go/src/pr-reviews/tmp/lws-1086/test/e2e/upgrade/upgrade_test.go#L403-L406), assert `if !isPodReady(&pod)` (or include the standalone `existing-lws` headless Service in `GeneratedService` in `captureSnapshot()`) so that both pod readiness and headless Service preservation across the `v0.7.0 -> current` upgrade are explicitly verified.
